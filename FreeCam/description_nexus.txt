[size=5][b]Free Camera (Native Spectator)[/b][/size]

A client-side mod for Palworld that detaches the camera component from the player character during construction and dismantling, allowing you to fly freely and build/dismantle without collision constraints within Base Camp boundaries.

[hr][/hr]

[size=4][b]Key Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*] [b]Free Flight Construction & Dismantling[/b]: Build and dismantle freely around your base camp by detaching the camera from your character.
[*] [b]Collision-Free Building[/b]: No more struggling with character collisions while trying to place structures.
[*] [b]Gamepad & Keyboard Support[/b]: Full support for both Keyboard and Gamepad flight, elevation (Fly Up/Down), and mode toggling.
[*] [b]ModConfigurator Integration[/b]: In-game GUI configuration support for keybinds and speed settings.
[*] [b]Adjustable Flight Speed[/b]: Change camera flight speed on the fly using [b]Page Up[/b] / [b]Page Down[/b] keys.
[*] [b]Extended Dismantle & Snap Range[/b]: Snapping and dismantling distances are increased by 15x while in FreeCam, allowing you to remodel your base easily from high above.
[*] [b]Dynamic Reticle Placement[/b]: Construction objects and dismantle targets can be placed/selected precisely at the reticle instead of locking onto the player character.
[*] [b]Base-Camp Restricted Activation[/b]: Activation is restricted to base camps only to prevent accidental out-of-bounds spectator triggers.
[*] [b]Horizontal Boundary Limits[/b]: Restricts flight movement to a horizontal 1.5x base camp radius cylinder centered on your character to prevent getting lost, with infinite vertical height limits.
[*] [b]Optimized Performance[/b]: Zero runtime table allocations in the tick loop, ensuring zero frame stutters or garbage collection lag during camera movement.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[size=4][b]How It Works[/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*] Press [b]Alt + F8[/b] (Keyboard) or [b]Gamepad LT + Start[/b] (Gamepad) while within your Base Camp boundaries.
[*] The mod automatically disables character collision and detaches the camera, entering Free Camera (Spectator) mode.
[*] Fly the camera using your movement keys/analog stick. Your character model will be hidden and teleported to follow your look location.
[*] Press [b]Alt + F8[/b] (Keyboard) or [b]Gamepad LT + Start[/b] (Gamepad) to exit Free Camera mode, re-attach the camera, re-enable collision, and return to your original position.
[/list]

[i]Note: Keyboard and Gamepad bindings can be fully customized in [b]%LOCALAPPDATA%/Pal/Saved/Mods/FreeCam/config.json[/b].[/i]

[hr][/hr]
