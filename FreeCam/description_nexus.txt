[size=5][b]Free Camera (Native Spectator)[/b][/size]

A client-side mod for Palworld that detaches the camera component from the player character during construction, allowing you to fly freely and build without collision constraints within Base Camp boundaries.

[hr][/hr]

[size=4][b]Key Features[/b][/size]

[list]
[*] [b]Free Flight Construction[/b]: Build freely around your base camp by detaching the camera from your character.
[*] [b]Collision-Free Building[/b]: No more struggling with character collisions while trying to place structures.
[*] [b]Auto Toggle[/b]: Automatically enables spectator mode when selecting a construction blueprint inside your base camp.
[*] [b]Dynamic Placement[/b]: Construction objects can be placed precisely at the reticle instead of locking onto the player character.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[size=4][b]How It Works[/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*] Select a construction blueprint while within your Base Camp boundaries.
[*] The mod automatically disables character collision and detaches the camera, entering Free Camera (Spectator) mode.
[*] Fly the camera using your standard movement keys. Your character model will be hidden and teleported to follow your look location.
[*] Deselect the blueprint to automatically re-attach the camera, re-enable collision, and return to your original position.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]

[list]
[*] [b]UE4SS[/b] (Compatible with the latest Palworld version)
[/list]
