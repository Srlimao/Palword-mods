**Description**  
Accessory Toggler is a highly optimized client-side Lua mod for Palworld that allows you to instantly equip and unequip your accessories using customizable hotkeys. 
Tired of opening your inventory every time you want to swap out your Ring of Mercy or temperature-resistant gear? This mod solves that by providing quick-toggle shortcuts and a sleek, customizable on-screen HUD that shows your currently active accessories.

**Main features**  
- **Instant Hotkey Toggling:** Quickly toggle your accessory slots 1 through 4 using customizable hotkeys (Default: `Alt + 5`, `Alt + 6`, `Alt + 7`, `Alt + 8`).
- **Sleek HUD Overlay:** Displays a clean, non-intrusive on-screen UI showing your currently equipped accessories and their active/disabled status.
- **HUD Edit Mode:** Press `Alt + F7` to enter Edit Mode. Use the Arrow Keys to move the UI around your screen, and `+` / `-` to adjust its scale. Press `Alt + R` to instantly reset it to the default position.
- **Auto-State Saving:** The mod automatically remembers your HUD position, scale, and which accessories you had disabled, restoring them seamlessly when you load back into the game.
- **Fully Localized:** Automatically uses the game's internal data tables to display accessory names in your chosen language.
- **Highly Optimized:** Designed with performance in mind. Uses background timers to scan your inventory, ensuring zero frame-rate drops or stuttering.

**Installation instructions**  
1. Download and install **UE4SS** (Unreal Engine 4 Scripting System) if you haven't already.
2. Download the Accessory Toggler mod `.zip` file.
3. Extract the contents of the `.zip` into your `Palworld\Pal\Binaries\Win64\Mods` folder.
4. Open the `mods.txt` file in the `Mods` folder and ensure `AccessoryToggler : 1` is present and enabled.
5. Launch Palworld and enjoy!

**Requirements**  
- **UE4SS (Unreal Engine 4 Scripting System)** is required to run this Lua-based mod. Ensure you are using a version compatible with the current Palworld patch.
