# PresetSwitch - Quick Pal Party Presets

Instantly switch between your saved Pal party presets using quick hotkeys (**Alt+5, Alt+6, Alt+7, Alt+8, Alt+9**)—without opening any menus!

### Key Features
- ⚡ **Instant Hotkey Switching**: Press **Alt + 5 / 6 / 7 / 8 / 9** to switch directly to your saved party presets.
- 🚫 **No Menus Needed**: Switch Pal teams on the fly while exploring, fighting, or flying without interrupting gameplay.
- 🎮 **Uses Game Presets**: Automatically connects to the party presets you save in your game.
- 🔔 **On-Screen Popup**: A brief notification confirms your active preset whenever you switch.
- ⚙️ **Fully Configurable**: Easily change keybinds or modifier keys (Alt, Ctrl, Shift, or None) in your config file.
